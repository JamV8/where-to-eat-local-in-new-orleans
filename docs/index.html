Doubt

How far can you trust a star rating?

A rating is only what the people who wrote reviews happened to think. Ask a different crowd and it shifts. So I let the lights flicker: the fewer reviews behind a rating, the more it shakes.

A place with can’t sit still.

The shake test

So I shook the top ten.

For every restaurant I draw a new set of reviews out of its own reviews: the same number, repeats allowed. Then I rank all again. You’re watching that happen. A ring means the place made the top ten on this draw.
